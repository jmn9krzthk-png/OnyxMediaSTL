Onyx Media makes video and runs ads for **home service companies** in St. Louis and St. Charles County: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, remodeling, landscaping, garage doors, pest control. The brand has to say two things at once: **this is premium work**, and **these people get it done**. Everything below serves that.

## Who we're talking to

- **The owner who built it.** Usually a man aged 30 to 60 who started on the tools and now runs 2 to 30 trucks. Revenue roughly $500K to $10M.
- **He's been burned before.** Agencies have sold him "brand awareness" and monthly reports nobody reads. He trusts specifics, proof and people who show up.
- **He's busy and on his phone.** He reads between jobs, in the truck, at 9pm. Short lines, big type, one clear next step.
- **Confidence comes from competence, not machismo.** Show the craft, the crew and the numbers. No flexing trucks for the sake of it, no "beast mode" talk, no stereotypes.

## Voice

Talk like a good foreman: direct, specific, respectful of his time.

- **Lead with the job outcome.** Booked calls, filled schedules, crews that look like the best in town. Gear and process come second.
- **Specific beats impressive.** Write "one shoot day, 12 reels" or "24-hour turnaround". Never write "high-quality content solutions".
- **Short sentences. Active voice.** If a line works without an adjective, cut the adjective.
- **"You" is the owner. "We" is Onyx.** Max signs emails and the About page personally.
- **Use his words.** Say jobs, calls, trucks, crew, service area, slow season, estimates and reviews. Avoid "engagement", "brand storytelling", "synergy", "elevate".
- **No emoji, no exclamation points, no hype words** ("game-changer", "next level", "passionate", "world-class").
- **Never invent proof.** Every number, client name and quote is real or it doesn't ship.

**Headlines that fit**
- More booked jobs from the work you already do.
- Your trucks are all over St. Louis. Your content should be too.
- We film your crew on real jobs. You get a month of reels.
- Look like the company people call first.

**Headlines that don't**
- Elevate your brand with cinematic storytelling.
- We're passionate about helping businesses grow!

**Casing.** Display headlines and eyebrows are uppercase in the type style. Write them in sentence case in the source and let CSS uppercase them. Buttons, nav and body copy use sentence case. The one fixed CTA is title case: "Book a Free Strategy Call".

**CTAs.** Primary: **Book a Free Strategy Call**. Secondary: **See Our Work**. Don't invent new primary CTAs page by page.

## Logo

Use the files in the Logos group exactly as supplied.

- **Primary** (`onyx-media-logo-primary.png`, onyx wordmark + amber MEDIA): on `bg` in the Daylight theme, on white paper, on light photos.
- **Reversed** (`onyx-media-logo-reversed.png`, off-white wordmark + amber MEDIA): on `bg` in the Onyx theme, on dark video frames, on truck wraps and shirts in dark colors.
- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the O on every side.
- **Minimum size:** 120px wide on screen, 1 inch wide in print. Below that, MEDIA becomes unreadable.
- **Don't:** recolor the amber; place the logo on an `amber` fill (the play triangle disappears); stretch, outline, add shadows, or put it over a busy photo without a dark scrim; retype the wordmark in a font.
- **The play triangle** inside the X is the brand's signature. It can stand alone as a graphic motif (see Graphic motif), but it is not a substitute logo.

## Color

Onyx black is the brand. Amber is the spark.

- **Proportions:** roughly 70% `bg` / `surface`, 20% `ink`, 10% or less `amber`. If a screen looks orange, there's too much amber.
- **Onyx theme is the default** for the website, ads, reel end cards and social graphics. Use **Daylight** for proposals, invoices, print and the occasional light section for rhythm.
- `amber` is for: the primary button fill, the play triangle, and **one** highlighted word in a headline on onyx. Never for paragraphs.
- Amber as text uses `amber-text`. Logo amber on white is only 2:1, so Daylight swaps in the burnt `#8F5300`.
- Text on amber is always `on-amber` (onyx black), never white.
- `success` and `danger` appear only in forms, always with a word ("Sent", "Add your phone number").
- **Avoid:** gradients, neon, blue accents, purple anything.

## Typography

One family, **Archivo**, carries the whole brand. Its wide, expanded cuts echo the geometric, wide-set logo.

- **Display** (`display-xl`, `display-l`, `display-m`): Archivo at width 125% (112% for `display-m`), weight 800–900, uppercase. Set big and tight. Display styles are the "bold and confident" voice, so use them for headlines only.
- **Text** (`heading`, `body-l`, `body`, `button`): Archivo at normal width. Body never below 16px; keep lines under about 65 characters.
- **Eyebrow** (`eyebrow`): 13px bold, 0.32em tracking, uppercase in `amber-text`. It mirrors the spaced-out MEDIA in the logo. Put one above every section headline.
- **Caption** (`caption`, JetBrains Mono): timecodes and video specs only, as a small film-set detail. No more than one per section.
- **Phone sizes:** `display-xl` drops to 56px and `display-l` to 40px.
- Load from Google Fonts: `Archivo:wdth,wght@62..125,400..900` and `JetBrains Mono:wght@500`.

## Graphic motif

The **play triangle** from the logo's X is the only graphic device.

- Use it as a bullet in lists, the arrow inside the primary button, a section marker, and a corner mark on video thumbnails.
- It is always solid `amber` (or `on-amber` inside an amber button) and always points right.
- Don't add other icon styles next to it. Where a UI icon is required (menu, close, phone), use simple 2px-stroke line icons in `ink`. The source has no icon set, so pick one set, use it everywhere, and note the choice here.

## Layout

- **Square corners.** `radius-0` everywhere. Buttons and inputs get `radius-sm` (2px). `radius-pill` is for filter chips only.
- **No shadows.** Separate things with `line` borders and `surface` steps.
- **Generous section rhythm:** `space-24` top and bottom on desktop, `space-16` on phones. Side gutter is `space-4` minimum on phones, `space-8` on desktop.
- **Left-aligned.** Headlines and copy align left. Only the final CTA band may center.
- **Video leads.** The hero is a full-bleed or letterboxed showreel loop. Case studies show the vertical reel first.

## Imagery and video

Real jobs, real crews, real St. Louis. No stock photos, ever.

- **Shoot:** techs on a rooftop condenser at golden hour; a plumber under a sink with a headlamp; roofers on the pitch; the crew lined up by the trucks at 7am; the owner talking straight to camera in his shop.
- **Grade:** deep blacks with detail kept in the shadows, warm highlights that sit near `amber`, natural skin. Avoid heavy teal-and-orange.
- **Formats:** 9:16 for reels and stories (the default), 2.39:1 for brand films and the website hero, 1:1 or 4:5 for feed ads.
- **On-screen text:** Archivo 800, uppercase, `ink` on a `bg` box. One word in `amber` at most. Captions burned in, because most people watch muted.
- **End card:** reversed logo on `bg`, the phone number, and "Book a free strategy call".
- **Website hero video:** muted autoplay loop of 30–60 seconds with a poster image and a static fallback for reduced motion.

## Motion

- Fast and decisive: 150–250ms, ease-out. Hover changes color and border only. Nothing bounces.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: stop the hero loop and show the poster.

## Accessibility

- Text pairs hold at least 4.5:1 in both themes; see each token's note.
- Every interactive element shows a 2px `focus` ring with a 3px offset.
- Form errors pair `danger` with a sentence that says how to fix it.

## Using this on the website

- Load `tokens.css`, then `components/bundle.css`.
- Set `data-theme="dark"` on `<html>`. Use `data-theme="light"` on a section for a Daylight block.
- Build pages from: **Button**, **Eyebrow**, **ServiceCard**, **CaseStudy**, **LeadForm**, **Logo**.
- **Homepage hero for home services:** eyebrow "HOME SERVICE VIDEO · ST. LOUIS", `display-xl` headline, `body-l` subhead, primary + secondary buttons, showreel below.
- **Service-by-industry cards:** HVAC & plumbing, roofing & exteriors, remodeling, landscaping & outdoor, garage doors & pest. Each card names the job outcome and the content we make for it.
