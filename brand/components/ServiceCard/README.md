# ServiceCard
A card that sells one service or one industry by its job outcome, with what's included and a starting price.

- **You provide:** an eyebrow (service or trade), a `display-m` outcome headline, a one-line description, 3–4 included items, and a footer with price ("From $[X]/mo") and "Details".
- The whole card is the link. Hover turns the border `amber`.
- Lay them out in a 2-column grid on desktop and 1 column on phones. Use the same number of list items on sibling cards.
- Never show a price you haven't confirmed. Use "From $[X]" until you have.
