    Personal accounts on these apps have no business capture route. TextGuard flags messages that try to move a conversation onto them and reports enrolled phones that have them installed.

    Enroll an advisor's phone

    Creates a single-use code (valid 72 hours) that the advisor types into the TextGuard app. The app shows the recording disclosure and the advisor must accept it before capture starts.

    Record integrity

    Every captured message and every review is appended to a hash-chained log. Editing, deleting or reordering any record breaks the chain at that point. There is no edit or delete function.
