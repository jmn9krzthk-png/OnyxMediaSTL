FAQ

Questions from locksmiths.

Do you work with locksmiths in St. Louis?

Yes. We create content and run ads for locksmiths across St. Louis, St. Charles County and nearby areas. Our studio is in St. Charles.

Why does a locksmith need video?

People are wary of who they let in. A real face, a real truck and a local shop on camera set you apart from faceless lead-generation listings.

What content works best for locksmiths?

Smart lock installs: Upgrades homeowners can see. Security tips: Simple ways to protect a home. Meet your locksmith: A real local face.

What does it cost for locksmiths?

Most locksmiths usually start with Essential content at $795/mo plus Google ads at $1,150/mo and a $625 one-time setup. Content plans run $795 to $2,195 a month, and you pay ad spend directly to the platforms.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. You pay ad spend directly to Meta, Google or Bing. Our fee covers planning, building and managing the ads.
