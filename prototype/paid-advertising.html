FAQ

Paid ads questions.

Which platforms do you run?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google. We pick based on how your customers search for you.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your market and goals. We recommend a starting ad budget on the call; ad spend is paid directly to the platform.

How do I know it's working?

Every lead goes to your email and a Google Sheet, and we report cost per lead and booked calls monthly.

Do I need to be on a content plan?

No, but ads perform best with fresh creative. Most clients run both.
