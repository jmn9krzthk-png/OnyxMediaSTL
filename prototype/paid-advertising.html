FAQ

Paid ads questions.

Where can you run my ads?

Facebook and Instagram, Google and Bing search, or all of them combined.

Is my ad budget included?

No. Your ad budget is paid directly to the platforms, so every dollar goes to your ads.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes, a one-time setup when we start. The calculator includes it in your first month.

Do I need a content plan too?

No, but ads perform best with fresh creative, so most clients run both.

Is there a long-term contract?

Most plans are month to month. Ask about current specials.
