Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

Onyx Media is a brand of ONYX HTZ LLC, based in St. Charles, Missouri ("Onyx Media," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through onyxmediastl.com and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you fill out a form or book a call, we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, company, business type, budget, timeline and message. When you book through Calendly, Calendly collects your booking details under its own privacy policy.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics or advertising tools we use (such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel) may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser, device, pages visited and how you arrived at our site. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies.

How we use information

To respond to your inquiry, schedule calls, provide quotes and deliver our services.

To understand how our website is used and to improve it.

To measure and improve our advertising, including showing ads to people who have visited our site.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (for example website hosting, form handling, scheduling, email, analytics and advertising platforms), or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you've given us by contacting us. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of interest-based ads through your Google and Meta ad settings.

Data retention and security

We keep inquiry information as long as needed to respond to you and run our business, and we use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
