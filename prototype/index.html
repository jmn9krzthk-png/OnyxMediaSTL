Onyx Media is Max Fisher's studio. We film, edit and run ads for home service companies and automotive businesses across St. Louis and St. Charles County.

How we work

The person who plans your shoot is the person behind the camera and the person reading your ad numbers. Nothing gets lost between teams, because there's only one.

Why it works for the trades

We shoot around your schedule, not ours. Shoot days happen on real jobs, so your crew keeps working while we roll. Homeowners trust what they can see: your trucks, your techs, your finished work.

We understand how shops and dealers sell: trust in the tech, transparency on the repair, and the car looking its best on camera. We film in bays, on the lot and on the road without getting in the way of the work.
