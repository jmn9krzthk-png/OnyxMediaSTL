FAQ

Questions from flooring companies.

Do you work with flooring companies in St. Louis?

Yes. We create content and run ads for flooring companies across St. Louis, St. Charles County and nearby areas. Our studio is in St. Charles.

Can you film in our showroom?

Yes. Showroom walkthroughs and product close-ups pair well with on-site install footage.

What content works best for flooring companies?

Room reveals: Old carpet out, new floors in. Material comparisons: Which floor fits which home. Install time-lapses: A room in thirty seconds.

What does it cost for flooring companies?

Most flooring companies usually start with Growth content at $1,495/mo plus Meta ads at $1,150/mo and a $625 one-time setup. Content plans run $795 to $2,195 a month, and you pay ad spend directly to the platforms.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. You pay ad spend directly to Meta, Google or Bing. Our fee covers planning, building and managing the ads.
