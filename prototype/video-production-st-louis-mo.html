FAQ

St. Louis questions.

Do you film in St. Louis?

Yes. We shoot on location in St. Louis and across the St. Louis metro. Our studio is in St. Charles.

What kinds of St. Louis businesses do you work with?

Home service companies (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, remodeling, landscaping, garage doors, pest control) and automotive businesses (repair shops, dealers, detailers, body shops).

How much does it cost?

Monthly content plans are $795, $1,395 or $2,195 a month. Ad management is $1,150 or $1,950 a month plus a one-time setup, and you pay ad spend directly to the platforms.

How fast can you start?

First posts can go live as soon as the next week.

Is there a contract?

Most plans are month to month. A special offer may come with a set term, stated before signing. Ask about current specials.
